Policy Name: Acceptable Use of Generative AI
Effective Date: September 1, 2026
Department: Office of Information Technology (OIT)

1. Overview
The University encourages the ethical exploration of Generative AI. However, the use of public AI tools (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or public Copilot endpoints) presents significant data security risks.

2. Data Restrictions
Faculty, staff, and student workers are strictly prohibited from entering Tier 1 (Highly Restricted) or Tier 2 (Confidential) data into any public AI prompt. This includes, but is not limited to:
- Student grades, ID numbers, or financial aid information (FERPA-protected).
- Unpublished institutional research data.
- Employee performance reviews or HR records.

3. Grading and Assessment
Instructors may not use public AI platforms to grade student essays or exams. Any automated grading must be conducted using the University's approved, on-premise "Secure Campus AI" environment. 

4. Syllabus Requirement
All faculty must include a clear AI usage statement in their syllabi by the second week of classes, explicitly stating whether AI tools are permitted for coursework.
