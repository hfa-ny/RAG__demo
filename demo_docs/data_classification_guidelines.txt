Policy Name: Institutional Data Classification Guidelines
Effective Date: January 15, 2025
Department: Data Governance Board

The University classifies all digital data into three tiers to dictate how it must be stored and processed.

Tier 1: Highly Restricted
Definition: Data protected by state or federal law (e.g., FERPA, HIPAA, PCI-DSS).
Examples: Social Security Numbers, student transcripts, medical records, credit card numbers.
Storage: Must be stored exclusively on local University servers or the strictly configured AWS GovCloud environment. 

Tier 2: Confidential
Definition: Internal data not meant for public distribution, but not strictly bound by federal privacy laws.
Examples: Internal departmental budgets, unreleased press statements, building security blueprints.
Storage: May be stored in the University's enterprise OneDrive or internal SharePoint sites.

Tier 3: Public
Definition: Information explicitly created for public consumption.
Examples: Course catalogs, published faculty directories, campus maps.
Storage: Can be hosted on public web servers or shared freely.
