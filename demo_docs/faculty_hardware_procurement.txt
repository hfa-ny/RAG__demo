Policy Name: Faculty Hardware Procurement and Refresh Cycle
Effective Date: May 10, 2026
Department: Academic Affairs & Purchasing

1. Standard Equipment Allocation
All full-time tenure-track faculty and full-time lecturers are eligible for a primary computing device upon hire. The maximum allocation for a standard laptop or desktop is $1,850. 

2. Refresh Cycle
Primary computing devices are eligible for replacement every four (4) years. Faculty must submit a hardware refresh request to the IT Helpdesk no later than April 1st of their eligibility year.

3. Specialized Hardware
Faculty in the Computer Science, Engineering, and Media Arts departments may request a "High-Performance Workstation" allocation up to $3,200. This requires a signed justification form from the Department Chair.

4. Peripheral Equipment
A secondary budget of $300 per faculty member is available every two years for peripherals, including external monitors, keyboards, mice, and approved webcams. 

5. Return of Old Equipment
Upon receiving a new device during a refresh cycle, the faculty member must return their old device to the IT Asset Management office within 14 business days.
